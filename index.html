Frequently asked questions

Answers from your flyer Rules + common client questions. Edit this section directly in index.html.

Are walk-ins allowed? +
No. All sessions are strictly by appointment. Please book via WhatsApp 07038563822 at least a day ahead. Walk-ins aren't guaranteed a slot.
How do I secure my booking? +
A 50% advance deposit secures your slot. Send proof of payment on WhatsApp, and your time is locked in. Balance is paid at your session.
What if I'm late or miss my slot? +
Please stick to your booked time. Defaulting on your booked time attracts a ₦5,000 fee as stated in our house rules. If you will be late, message us early.
Are payments refundable? +
No. Deposits and payments are non-refundable once a slot is booked. You can reschedule with 24h notice without losing deposit.
How do I pay? +
Bank transfer to:
Wema Bank – 0245817735 – Hanni Beauty Palace
Access Bank – 0031605726 – Akomolafe Anu
Send receipt on WhatsApp. No POS yet.
Where are you located exactly? +
Adebiyi Shopping Complex, Kuforiji Olubi Junction, Onikoko Road, Adigbe, Abeokuta, Ogun State. Inside the complex, upstairs. Use the map above and the Get Directions button.
Do you sell products online? +
Yes! Add items to bag in the Shop section and checkout — pay securely online (card, bank transfer or USSD via Paystack), choose delivery or pickup, and we'll confirm your order.
What products and services do you offer? +
Organic Skincare, Makeup, Selling of Cosmetics, Lash & Microblading, Pedicure & Manicure, Facials, Body Massage & Spa, Body Enhancement, Aphrodisiacs/Kayamata, Perfume & Body Spray. All organic, vegan, cruelty-free where possible.
How do I get to the store from Adigbe? +
From Adigbe junction, take Onikoko Road to Kuforiji Olubi Junction. Adebiyi Complex is on your right. 2 mins from Adigbe market. Okada or Keke: say “Adebiyi Complex, Kuforiji Olubi”.
What are your working hours and fastest contact? +
By appointment only. WhatsApp is fastest: 07038563822. Calls: 08120428466. Instagram DM: @hanni_beautyplace. We reply within minutes during 9am–7pm.
